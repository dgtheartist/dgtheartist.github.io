About

DG the Artist is a DMV-based rapper and hyperpop artist known for his electrifying stage presence, his wholesome but existential lyrics, and his raw energy that shines through on every track. Originally renowned for his world-class Rubik's Cube speedsolving and YouTube content under the name DGCubes, he now brings the same dedication and passion to music that he was known for in the cubing world.

His music tackles themes from his struggles with OCD to his strong stance on veganism. After the success of his 2025 album You Deserve The World, DG pivoted hard, quitting his software engineering job to pursue a career in music full-time. In 2026, he released his debut EP as a full-time rapper, I QUIT MY DAY JOB, which he describes as a celebration of his exit from the corporate world.

With the help of his devoted fanbase online and in-person, DG is making his dreams come true, performing across the country from Washington DC to Washington state. Wherever you find DG the Artist, he'll make sure you know that You Deserve The World.

Music

I QUIT MY DAY JOB

EP ~ 2026

"I QUIT MY DAY JOB" is a five-song celebration of DG's exit from the corporate world to finally pursue music full-time. Featuring a mix of hard-hitting rap verses, energizing melodies, and unforgettable features, this victory lap of an EP ushers in a new era of DG the (full-time) Artist.

I DON'T WANT POTENTIAL!!

Single ~ 2026

"I DON'T WANT POTENTIAL!!" is the most "DG" song yet. The track slaps you in the face with hyperpop-inspired vocals over an extremely fast-paced instrumental, followed by a whiplash-inducing beat switch to close out the song with some classic introspective rap from DG.

Phú Quốc

Single ~ 2025

This vibey summer song was written on a relaxing vacation to the Vietnamese island of Phú Quốc. Lyrically, DG explores the theme of wanting to disappear from society and spend the rest of his life chilling with his wife. This is DG's first single after the release of his 2025 album "You Deserve The World," and he sees it almost like a bonus track for that album.

You Deserve The World

Album ~ 2025

"You Deserve The World" is an addictively energetic and surprisingly vulnerable rap album about finding the balance between pursuing your dreams and experiencing the outdoors. It's a mixture of vibey introspective hip-hop and mind-breaking autotune-drenched hyperpop that you won't find anywhere else. Join DG on his journey to experience everything the world has to offer, because You Deserve The World.

What Do You Want?!

Single ~ 2024

This song is all of DG's chaotic energy bottled up into a two-minute fever dream. Much like peanut butter on a banana, or mango on more mango, this track's unique blend of rap and hyperpop brings out the best of both worlds. DG was inspired to release this song after previewing it at two live performances to amazing reactions from crowds, and he's been riding that high ever since.

SKATE

Single ~ 2024

"SKATE" is an intensely charismatic trap anthem by DG the Artist. Alluding to his occasional desire to drop all responsibilities and "skate" by in life, the hook is instantly memorable and gets audiences chanting along. The verses slowly ramp up in intensity, building up to a climax that leaves listeners with too much energy to know what to do with.

GET MONEY

Single ~ 2023

A track that sounds like it came straight out of a futuristic industrial rave, "GET MONEY" fuses hip-hop and hyperpop to create an undeniably earwormy banger. This hit serves as a follow-up to DG's previous hyper-trap endeavors "GET IT NOW" and "GET UP" and takes the style to a truly extreme level of rule-breaking, autotune-drenched anthemic music. The song starts out with a high-energy trap hook and transitions into a desperately emotional punk-inspired verse to keep you moving from start to finish.

FIVE THOUSAND DOLLAR BOAT

Single ~ 2023

Coming off his sophomore album "Ego Death," DG returns with a lighthearted summer track that will leave listeners questioning "What kind of boat can you even get for five thousand dollars?" With a uniquely enunciated delivery across lots of creative punchlines and an earworm of a hook, this track will bring immaculate vibes the next time you get the aux.

Ego Death

Album ~ 2023

Where "Hello World" was an introduction, "Ego Death" is an immersion – an intimate exploration of the conscience, delving deep into the subconscious realm in a desperate search for peace. This is a concept album that follows DG the Artist through a quest to kill his ego and live happily in the moment. From the egoistic intro "SPACESHIP" to the heartfelt finale of "Still Alive," with plenty of memorable moments and featured artists along the way, "Ego Death" is a truly cohesive and unforgettable piece of art.

Hello World

Album ~ 2022

DG the Artist's freshman project "Hello World" is DG's introduction to the music industry through an exciting catalog of seven polished tracks. Each song has its own unique style inspired by DG's personal musical preferences, leaving the listener with a fleshed out experience where no two songs sound alike.
